**Titel:** InfoBar — Boss Bar Messages [1.21 - 26.2]
**Tagline:** Persistent, animated boss bars for server info, playtime, TPS or any text — as many bars as you want, each with its own style.
**Kern:** Old-school boss-bar-message plugins on Spigot pulled tens of thousands of downloads but sit abandoned since 2022/2023, with nothing comparable on Modrinth. InfoBar is a modern rebuild for Paper 1.21+ and 26.x, built on the Adventure BossBar API: unlimited named bars, MiniMessage/legacy text, live placeholders ({player} {online} {max} {tps} {world} {time}), animation frames, per-bar color/overlay/progress, time-of-day progress mode, per-player toggle. Zero dependencies. Actively maintained for every new Minecraft version.

Human-led development: a human maintainer directs, tests and takes responsibility for every release; AI is used as a development tool.
**Tags:** utility, hud, bossbar, information, server
